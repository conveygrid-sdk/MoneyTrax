## 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Where is my data stored?
All your financial entries (expenses, incomes, budgets, categories, and payment notes) are stored **exclusively on your local device**. MoneyTrax never transmits your financial transactions to remote servers.

### How do I delete my profile and all data?
You can delete all stored records locally at any time:
1. Open **MoneyTrax**.
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Delete Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete All Data** and confirm.

### How do I request deletion of consent records?
To delete contact records (Full Name, Email, Mobile Number) recorded during consent verification, send an email to **kcspldoc@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request - MoneyTrax"*. We will verify and process your request within 30 calendar days.

### Does MoneyTrax have ads or tracking?
No. MoneyTrax is ad-free and contains no tracking or analytics SDKs.
