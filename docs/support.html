Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

💡 Where is my expense and income data stored?
MoneyTrax is built with a local-first privacy architecture. All your financial records—including expense amounts, notes, income logs, payment methods, and budgets—are stored strictly on your local device. No financial transaction data is uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.
🌐 Does MoneyTrax require an active internet connection to track expenses?
No. Daily expense and income recording, budget tracking, and charts work completely offline. Network connectivity is only utilized during initial profile setup to verify and register statutory regulatory consent.
🚫 Are there any ads or tracking in the app?
No. MoneyTrax contains no advertisements, no ad networks (such as Google AdMob), and no user tracking/analytics SDKs.
🗑️ How do I delete my profile and all stored data?
You can delete all your stored data at any time directly in the app:
  1. Open MoneyTrax.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Profile.
  3. Tap Delete All Data and confirm.
This will permanently and irreversibly wipe all local expenses, income records, categories, and profile details from your device.
✉️ How do I request deletion of consent records?
To request deletion of your contact information (Name, Email, Mobile Number) recorded during consent verification, send an email to kcspldoc@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - MoneyTrax". Our team will verify and process your request within 30 days.
💱 How do I change my currency symbol?
You can change your preferred currency symbol (₹, $, €, £, ¥, A$, C$) anytime by navigating to Settings → Currency within the app.
