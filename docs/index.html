1. Introduction

Welcome to MoneyTrax ("the Application", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by Rysun ("Company", "Developer"). We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding our data handling practices.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of MoneyTrax across supported mobile operating systems (including Google Android and Apple iOS). By accessing or using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Data Collection and Processing

We adhere strictly to data minimization principles. Below is a detailed breakdown of the types of data collected, why it is processed, and where it resides.

Data Category Specific Data Items Storage Location Purpose
Financial Data Expense amounts, income entries, categories, payment methods, transaction dates, and custom notes. Stored locally on device only (never transmitted). Core App Functionality: Personal budget planning, expense tracking, and generation of local reports.
Personal Identifiers Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number. Local device storage & transmitted to consent management service. Profile personalization & regulatory consent verification/auditing.
Preferences & Settings Preferred currency symbol (e.g., ₹, $, €), category customizations, and display themes. Stored locally on device only. App personalization and usability.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Services

MoneyTrax does not sell, license, or lease your personal information. Data transmission is limited to the single following operational requirement:

Consent Management Service (ConveyGrid / Sammati SDK)

To comply with digital data protection statutory mandates (such as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act / DPDP and global privacy frameworks), MoneyTrax integrates the Sammati Notice SDK provided by ConveyGrid.

  • Data Transmitted: User's Full Name, Email Address, and Mobile Number.
  • Purpose: Displaying statutory consent notices, recording express consent decisions, and maintaining verifiable digital consent artifacts.
  • Service Endpoint: Secure API gateway (https://conveygridapidev.rysun.in/) via TLS/HTTPS.
  • Independent Processing: ConveyGrid does not use this data for cross-app tracking, targeted marketing, or behavioral profiling.
What We DO NOT Do:
  • We do NOT use advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity, Meta).
  • We do NOT track your physical location (GPS or network cell data).
  • We do NOT use behavioral tracking or analytics frameworks (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer).
  • We do NOT access your phone address book, photos, camera, or microphone.

4. Device Permissions

MoneyTrax operates with minimal device privileges. The App only utilizes standard internet access solely for verifying and transmitting consent notices during profile creation. The App requires no runtime permissions for location, contacts, media, microphone, or camera.

5. Data Retention and Account/Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion policies, users have comprehensive control over the retention and deletion of their data:

A. Immediate In-App Deletion

You can permanently wipe all data stored on your device at any time:

  1. Navigate to the Settings tab inside MoneyTrax.
  2. Tap on Delete Profile.
  3. Confirm the prompt. All stored transactions, categories, budgets, and user profile data will be permanently and irreversibly removed from the device immediately.

B. Web Request for Consent Data Removal

If you wish to delete your contact information (Name, Email, Mobile Number) recorded during consent verification:

  • Submit an email request to kcspldoc@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - MoneyTrax".
  • Specify the registered email address and mobile number associated with your account.
  • Our privacy compliance team will verify your identity and purge your records within 30 calendar days.

6. Security of Your Information

We take data security seriously:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted to external services is encrypted using modern cryptographic protocols (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 over HTTPS).
  • Local Application Sandboxing: All financial entries are isolated within the operating system's application sandbox, shielded from unauthorized access by other applications installed on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

MoneyTrax is intended for general adult audiences and does not direct its services to children. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at kcspldoc@gmail.com, and we will promptly remove such information.
