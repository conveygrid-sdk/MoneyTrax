# Privacy Policy for MoneyTrax

**Last Updated:** September 22, 2026  
**Effective Date:** September 22, 2026

Welcome to **MoneyTrax** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by **Rysun** ("Developer", "Company"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use the MoneyTrax mobile application (`com.rysun.dailyexpensetracker`).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By downloading, accessing, or using MoneyTrax, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

## 1. Summary of Key Principles

- **Local-First Financial Storage:** All your daily financial records—including expenses, incomes, categories, budgets, and notes—are stored exclusively on your device. We do not store, view, sell, or transmit your financial transactions to our servers.
- **Minimal Personal Information:** We collect your Full Name, Email Address, and Mobile Number solely to create your local profile and record statutory consent.
- **Zero Advertising & Tracking:** MoneyTrax contains **no advertisements**, **no third-party ad SDKs**, **no analytics trackers**, and does not track your activity across other apps or websites.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete all your stored financial and profile data instantly from within the App at any time via **Settings → Delete Profile**, or request data deletion via email.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
When you register or set up your profile within the App, we collect the following personal identifiers:
- **Full Name** (or First Name)
- **Email Address**
- **Mobile / Phone Number**

**Purpose & Legal Basis:**
- To create and personalize your in-app profile.
- To fulfill regulatory consent requirements and maintain compliant consent artifacts under applicable data protection laws (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act / DPDP and GDPR guidelines).

### B. Financial and Transactional Information (Stored Locally Only)
When using the App's budgeting and tracking features, you may enter:
- Expense entries (amount, category, date, payment method, notes)
- Income entries (amount, income source, date, payment method, notes)
- Custom expense and income categories
- Monthly budget thresholds and currency preferences

**Important:** All financial transaction data is saved **locally on your device** using local storage frameworks (such as Apple SwiftData / local databases). This data is **never** uploaded to our servers, never backed up to third-party marketing servers, and never accessible by Rysun or any unauthorized third parties.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
- **Local App Preferences:** Currency choice, onboarding completion state, and UI theme preferences are stored locally on your device via standard local key-value storage (UserDefaults/local storage).
- **Device Information:** MoneyTrax does **not** collect IMEI, hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, GPS location, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or biometric data.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
1. **Core App Functionality:** Enabling you to manage personal finances, set budgets, view graphical expense breakdowns, and track income.
2. **Consent Lifecycle Management:** Verifying, logging, and managing user consent for regulatory transparency and audit trails via integrated consent management architecture.
3. **Customer Support:** Responding to inquiries, feedback, or support requests initiated by you.

We do **not** use your personal information for automated profiling, credit scoring, marketing, or behavioral advertising.

## 4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or lease your personal information. We only share information with third parties in the specific circumstances described below:

### Regulatory Consent Management: ConveyGrid / Sammati Notice SDK
- **Service Provider:** ConveyGrid / Sammati SDK (`https://conveygridapidev.rysun.in/`)
- **Data Processed:** Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Number, Timestamp, and Consent Status.
- **Purpose:** To present statutory privacy notices, capture explicit consent, and generate verifiable digital consent artifacts in accordance with data privacy regulations (e.g., DPDP Act).
- **Security:** All transmissions between the App and the consent management service are protected using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

### Other Third Parties
- **No Ad Networks:** We do not integrate Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, or any other advertising platforms.
- **No Analytics Providers:** We do not use third-party behavioral analytics tools (e.g., Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel, Adjust).
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information only if required to do so by applicable law, governmental inquiry, or valid legal process.

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All communications with the consent management service utilize HTTPS / TLS encryption.
- **Local Sandboxing:** Your financial records reside within the sandboxed application environment provided by your mobile operating system, inaccessible to other apps.
- **Access Restrictions:** We maintain strict role-based access controls for any support inquiries or backend administrative systems.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or mobile storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to protect your device with a secure passcode or biometric lock.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

### In-App Local Deletion
You have complete control over your data stored locally on your device. You can erase all data at any time:
1. Open **MoneyTrax**.
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Delete Profile**.
3. Confirm the deletion prompt.
4. All local profile records, expenses, income logs, budgets, and custom categories will be **permanently wiped** immediately.

### Deletion of Transmitted Consent Data
If you wish to request the deletion of your personal contact records (Name, Email, Phone number) held by the consent management service:
- **Email Request:** Send an email to **kcspldoc@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request - MoneyTrax"*.
- Please include the email address and mobile number used during registration so we can verify and locate your consent artifact.
- Requests will be acknowledged and processed within 30 days in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.
